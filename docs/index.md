# Q-Selector

Q-Selector helps users choose quantum processors and explore how well different
processors suit their circuits. It ranks candidates for OpenQASM 2 circuits using
circuit structure and backend calibration features.

Users can generate their own datasets and train models for their circuits and
processors. Start with
[Installation](installation.md) and the [Usage guide](quickstart.md).
